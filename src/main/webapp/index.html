System Documentation & Help Guides

Registering a Patient Appointment

  • Navigate to the "Register Appointment" tab on the sidebar.
  • Enter the patient's Full Name, 10-digit mobile contact number, and Home Address.
  • Select the assigned Dentist (consultation fees will update accordingly).
  • Select the Treatment Type (cleaning, extraction, braces, etc. have fixed costs).
  • Pick the Appointment Date and Time, then click "Register Appointment".
  • The system automatically executes a database check to ensure the dentist has no double bookings on the selected time, and queues an SMS confirmation alert.

Searching Appointment Bookings

  • Navigate to the "Search Appointment" tab.
  • Type in the Appointment Number (e.g. APT-0001) and hit Enter or click "Search".
  • The system queries the database and displays the patient profile, date/time, and appointment status (Pending, Completed, Cancelled).

Generating Bills and Invoicing

  • Navigate to the "Calculate & Bill" tab.
  • Input the Appointment Number (e.g., APT-0001) and click "Calculate".
  • Choose a pricing strategy: Standard, Loyalty Member (10% off Doctor Fee), or Senior Citizen (15% off Treatment Cost).
  • If required, specify an additional Manual Discount in LKR.
  • Observe the real-time calculated breakdown on screen.
  • Click "Save Invoice" to write the bill record to the database (this transitions the appointment status to "Completed").
  • Click "Record Payment" when the patient hands over cash/card to mark the invoice as "Paid".
  • Use "Print Receipt" to print a clean paper receipt for patient records.
