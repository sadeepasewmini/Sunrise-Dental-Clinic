Registering an Appointment
  • Go to "Register Appointment" in the sidebar.
  • Enter patient name, 10-digit contact, and address.
  • Select the assigned Dentist and Treatment type from dropdowns.
  • Choose the date and time, then click "Register Appointment".
  • The system will auto-block double bookings and queue an SMS alert.
Searching Appointments
  • Go to "Search Appointment" and enter the Appointment Number (e.g. APT-0001).
  • Full patient and appointment details will appear instantly.
Generating Bills & Receipts
  • Go to "Calculate & Bill" and enter the Appointment Number.
  • Select the billing strategy: Standard, Loyalty (10% off Doctor), or Senior Citizen (15% off Treatment).
  • Add a manual discount if needed. Breakdown updates in real-time.
  • Click "Save Invoice" → then "Mark Paid" when payment is received.
  • Use "Print Receipt" to print a physical copy for the patient.
